MATH

The only number that agrees with itself

Four is the smallest composite number, and the only one where addition and multiplication of its own halves land on the same answer: 2+2 = 2×2 = 4. It's also a perfect square (2²), which gives it a quiet distinction — perfect squares are the only numbers with an odd count of divisors, and 4 has exactly three: 1, 2, and 4.

It shows up as a boundary more than a quantity: four dimensions in spacetime, four colors to tile any map without two neighbors matching, four sides to close a shape with the fewest straight lines that isn't a triangle.

RIDDLE

The number that spells its own size

What number, spelled out, has exactly as many letters as its own value?

four → F-O-U-R → 4 letters

Start from almost any number, count the letters in its name, then count the letters in that result, and keep going — the chain almost always collapses into four and sticks there. Four is the fixed point English numbers fall into.
